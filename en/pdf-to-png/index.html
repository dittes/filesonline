When PNG is the better choice

PNG compresses without losing detail, so text, lines and diagrams stay perfectly sharp with none of the fuzzy edges JPG can leave. That makes it a good fit for slides in a presentation, charts in a report or images in documentation.

Choose the resolution and which pages to convert. PNG files are larger than JPG, and multiple pages download together as a ZIP.

How to convert PDF to PNG

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Choose the image quality and which pages to convert.

  3. Click “Convert to PNG” and download your images.

Frequently asked questions

Is the background transparent?

No. Pages are rendered on a white background, as they appear in a PDF viewer.

Why are PNG files so large?

PNG keeps every detail. Choose a lower resolution, or use PDF to JPG, if you need smaller files.

Can I convert only some pages?

Yes. List them in the Pages field, for example 1-3, 7.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
