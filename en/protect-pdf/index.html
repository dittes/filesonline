Real encryption, and only you know the password

Your PDF is encrypted with AES-256, the strongest standard the PDF format offers. Without the password its contents are unreadable, even if the file gets into the wrong hands. You can also restrict printing, copying and editing.

Many online tools see your password as well as your document. Here both stay on your device. That also means nobody can recover a forgotten password, so keep it somewhere safe.

How to password protect a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Enter a password twice and choose what readers may do.

  3. Click “Protect PDF” and download your encrypted file.

Why people use it

Your file never leaves your device

Other online PDF tools upload your document to their servers. This one does the work inside your browser, so there is nothing to intercept, store or delete later.

Free, with no limits to work around

No account, no email address, no daily quota and no watermark on the result. The only limit is what your device can handle.

Works anywhere you have a browser

Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone. Nothing to install, and once a page has loaded it keeps working without an internet connection.

Frequently asked questions

What if I forget the password?

The file cannot be opened without it, and nobody can recover it for you. Keep your unprotected original or store the password safely.

How strong is the protection?

AES-256 is very strong if your password is good. Use a long one that is not a common word.

Do the print and copy restrictions always work?

Restrictions depend on the reader software respecting them, and not every program does. The password to open the file is the real protection.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
