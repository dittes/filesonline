Everything you need to mark up a PDF

Add text anywhere with your choice of size and color, including all German, Polish and Czech characters. Highlight passages, draw boxes and freehand notes, insert logos or images, and use White-out to cover something up.

This editor adds new content on top of your pages, but it does not change the text already in the PDF. White-out only covers what is underneath, so use Redact PDF for anything confidential.

How to edit a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Pick a tool from the toolbar, then click or drag on the page. Select an item to move or resize it.

  3. Click “Save PDF” and download your edited file.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the existing text in a PDF?

Not directly. You can cover it with White-out and type new text on top.

Can I sign with this tool?

You can place an image of your signature here, but Sign PDF is made for it, with drawing and typing built in.

Is White-out safe for confidential information?

No. The text underneath stays in the file. Use Redact PDF, which removes it for good.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
