Sort photos into folders by date taken

Put a messy photo folder in order by the date each picture was taken. You see the plan before it is applied, and nothing leaves your device.

Real capture date

The date is read from EXIF in JPG files. Other files use their modification date, and the preview shows where each date came from.

Folders your way

Sort into folders by year (2026), by year and month (2026/2026-11) or by year, month and day.

Rename with the date

Optionally put the date and time in front of the name or use it as the whole name, such as 2026-11-14 18.30.05.jpg.

How to sort photos by date

  1. Click “Open folder” or “Open files”, or drop your photos onto this page. The file manager opens with the photo tool ready.

  2. Choose the folder layout and whether to rename files, then check the preview.

  3. Apply it and download the result as a ZIP. In Chrome and Edge you can also save it straight into a folder.

Where the date comes from

Cameras and phones write the moment of capture into the photo as EXIF data. That is more reliable than the file date, which changes when a file is copied or edited. This tool reads the EXIF date from JPG files.

Files without an EXIF date, such as PNG images or videos, fall back to the file's modification date. The preview marks which date was used for each file, so you can spot a wrong guess before you apply anything. Your original files are not moved or renamed.

Frequently asked questions

Does it move my original photos?

No. The result is built in a workspace and downloaded as a ZIP or saved to a folder. Your originals stay where they are.

What if a photo has no EXIF date?

The file's modification date is used instead, and the preview marks it so you can see it.

Can I choose how detailed the folders are?

Yes. You can sort by year, by year and month, or by year, month and day.

Can I rename the photos too?

Yes. You can add the date and time in front of the name or use it as the whole name.
