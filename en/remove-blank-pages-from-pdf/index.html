Clean up double-sided scans

Double-sided scanning is quick, but every page with an empty back adds a blank page to the file. This tool looks at each page, measures how much of it is printed on, and marks the empty ones for you.

Scans are rarely perfectly white, so you can choose how strict it should be: only completely empty pages, pages with a little scanner noise, or pages that hold just a page number or stamp. You see the marked pages before anything is removed and can change the selection with a click.

How to remove blank pages

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Check the pages marked as blank, and change how strict the search is if needed.

  3. Click “Remove blank pages” and download the cleaned-up file.

Frequently asked questions

How does it decide a page is blank?

It renders each page and measures the share of dark pixels, ignoring a thin border where scanners leave shadows.

What if it marks a page I want to keep?

Click the page to unmark it. Nothing is removed until you confirm.

Are pages with “intentionally left blank” removed?

With the default setting, usually yes, because a single short line counts as nearly empty. Choose “Only completely empty pages” to keep them.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
