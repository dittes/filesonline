Compare two PDFs

Find every word that was added, removed or changed between two versions of a document.

or drop it here

  • No upload
  • Free
  • No account
  • Works offline once loaded

Spot changes without reading twice

When a contract, offer or manuscript comes back, the hard part is finding what someone changed. This tool reads the text of both versions and lines them up word by word, so every addition and deletion shows up in a list with the words around it.

You can also download both PDFs with the changes marked: removed words in red in the original, added words in green in the new version. Contracts and drafts rarely belong on a stranger’s server, so the comparison runs entirely in your browser.

How to compare two PDFs

  1. Choose the original PDF and the changed version, or drop both at once.

  2. Look through the list of changes. Use “Swap” if the files are the wrong way round.

  3. Click “Download marked PDFs” to get both files with the changes highlighted.

Frequently asked questions

Does it find changes in images or layout?

No. It compares the text. A moved picture, a new colour or different spacing is not reported.

Can I compare scanned documents?

Yes, after you run OCR PDF on both scans so they contain text.

Does it work with long documents?

Yes. Hundreds of pages are fine as long as the versions are broadly similar. Two completely different documents cannot be lined up word by word.

Which file is the original?

The first one you choose. You can swap them at any time before downloading.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
