From fixed page to working spreadsheet

Bank statements, price lists and reports often only exist as PDFs. This tool detects columns from the position of the text and writes each page to its own sheet, recognizing numbers in both 1,234.50 and 1.234,50 notation.

It works best with simple tables made of text in clear columns. Tables with merged cells or scanned pages may need some tidying, and scans have to go through OCR PDF first.

How to convert PDF to Excel

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Click “Convert to Excel”.

  3. Download the .xlsx file and open it in Excel, LibreOffice or Google Sheets.

Why people use it

Your file never leaves your device

Other online PDF tools upload your document to their servers. This one does the work inside your browser, so there is nothing to intercept, store or delete later.

Free, with no limits to work around

No account, no email address, no daily quota and no watermark on the result. The only limit is what your device can handle.

Works anywhere you have a browser

Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone. Nothing to install, and once a page has loaded it keeps working without an internet connection.

Frequently asked questions

Will my table layout be kept?

Rows and columns are rebuilt from the text positions. Simple tables come out clean, while merged cells may need some adjusting.

Why is every page a separate sheet?

It keeps long documents easy to check against the original. You can combine the sheets in Excel afterwards.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Run OCR PDF first, because a scan has no text to read. The result then depends on the scan quality.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
