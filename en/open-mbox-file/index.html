What is an MBOX file?

        
MBOX is a common format for storing many emails in one file. You get one when you export your mail with Google Takeout, or back up a mailbox from Thunderbird or Apple Mail.
This viewer opens those archives right in your browser, even very large ones. You can search by sender, subject or date, follow conversations, read attachments and export what you need, while your email stays on your device.

      

    

    

    

      
How to open an MBOX file

      
  1. Click “Choose MBOX File” or drop your .mbox file onto the page. It is read locally on your device.
  2. Search and filter your email, for example from:alice has:attachment, and open any message to read it.
  3. Export single messages or a selection as EML, MBOX, CSV or a ZIP file.

    

  

    

    

      
Frequently asked questions

      

        
How do I open an MBOX file from Gmail?
Google Takeout gives you a .mbox file inside a ZIP archive. Unpack the ZIP, then open the .mbox file here. There is nothing to import and no account to connect.

        
Is my email uploaded?
No. The archive is read by your browser and stays on your device. Remote images in emails are blocked until you allow them, so opening a message does not notify the sender.

        
How large can the file be?
Archives of hundreds of megabytes work well. Multi-gigabyte archives depend on how much memory your device has, since the text is kept in memory for searching.

        
Can I convert MBOX to EML or CSV?
Yes. Select the messages you want and export them as EML files in a ZIP, as a new MBOX file, or as a CSV list.

        
Can I keep an archive for later?
Yes. You can save it in your browser's local storage so it opens instantly next time. It is still stored only on your device.
