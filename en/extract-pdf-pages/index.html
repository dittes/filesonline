Keep only the pages you need

Often you only need to send part of a document: the signature page, one chapter, a single invoice from a batch. See every page as a thumbnail, click the ones you want and save them as a new document.

Your original file stays exactly as it is. You can combine the selected pages into one PDF or save each one as a separate file.

How to extract pages from a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Click the pages you want to keep.

  3. Click “Extract pages” and download the new PDF.

Why people use it

Your file never leaves your device

Other online PDF tools upload your document to their servers. This one does the work inside your browser, so there is nothing to intercept, store or delete later.

Free, with no limits to work around

No account, no email address, no daily quota and no watermark on the result. The only limit is what your device can handle.

Works anywhere you have a browser

Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone. Nothing to install, and once a page has loaded it keeps working without an internet connection.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference from Split PDF?

Split cuts a whole document into parts. Extract lets you hand-pick pages from anywhere in it.

Can I change the order of the pages?

Pages are saved in document order. To reorder them, use Organize PDF.

Does the original file change?

No. Your original stays untouched and you download a new file.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
